## 💰 Section 2: Loan Calculations

### 2.1 EMI Calculation

**Formula:**  
```
EMI = P × r × (1+r)^n / [(1+r)^n - 1]

Where:
  P = Principal (Loan Amount) = ₹95,00,000
  r = Monthly Interest Rate = 7.15% / 12 = 0.5958%
  n = Total Months = 15 × 12 = 180
```

**Result:**
| Metric | Amount |
|--------|--------|
| **Monthly EMI** | **₹86,187** |
| Total Payment (15 years) | ₹1,55,13,722 |

### 6.2 Why Renting Wins Financially

1. **Higher Investment Returns**
   - SIP returns (12%) > Property appreciation (4%)
   - Equity compounds faster than real estate

2. **Lower Upfront Cost**
   - No ₹50 lakh locked in property
   - Full ₹40L working in high-return SIPs from day 1

3. **Flexibility & Liquidity**
   - Can access funds anytime
   - No maintenance, property tax, or selling hassles

4. **Rent is Still Affordable**
   - Starting rent (₹27k) is only 7.2% of income
   - Even after 10 years (₹41k), still just 11% of income

## 💡 Section 7: Final Recommendation

### 7.1 If You're Purely Focused on Wealth Maximization

**✅ CONTINUE RENTING**
- 35% higher net worth after 10 years
- More liquidity and flexibility
- Can access funds for emergencies or opportunities
- Disciplined SIP investing beats 4% property appreciation

**BUT:** You must have the discipline to:
1. Actually invest ₹86k/month in SIPs consistently
2. Not touch the money for 10 years
3. Handle landlord uncertainties and rent increases
4. Be okay with not owning an asset

### 7.2 If You Value Stability & Emotional Benefits

**✅ BUY THE PROPERTY**
- EMI is comfortable at 23% of income
- Reputed builder (Adarsh) and good location (Sarjapur)
- Own 54% of the property in 10 years
- Protection from rent increases and forced moves
- Peace of mind and sense of achievement

**Trade-off:** You'll have ₹85L less net worth, but:
- You own a ₹2.15 Cr asset
- No more rent payments after loan closure
- Can live rent-free in retirement

### 7.3 The Hybrid Approach (Best of Both Worlds?)

Consider this middle path:

1. **Buy the property** for stability
2. **Continue investing** ₹50-80k/month in SIPs
3. **Prepay loan aggressively** with bonuses/increments
4. Close the loan in 8-10 years instead of 15

This gives you:
- The emotional benefits of home ownership
- Continued wealth building through SIPs
- Faster loan closure = less interest paid
- A more balanced portfolio (property + equity)
