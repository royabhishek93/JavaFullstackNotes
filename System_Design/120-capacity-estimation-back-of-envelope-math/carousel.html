Right-sized design

A single Postgres instance handles ~10,000 read QPS all day. If your real peak is 500 QPS, you just proposed sharding for a problem you don't have. If it's 70,000 QPS, no amount of tuning saves one box.

The Solution — Step 1-3

The Estimation Funnel: DAU → Peak QPS

100M DAU × 2 posts/day → 200M writes/day
100M DAU × 20 reads/day → 2B reads/day
200M writes ÷ 86,400 sec → ~2,315 avg write QPS
2B reads ÷ 86,400 sec → ~23,150 avg read QPS
avg × 2-3x peak factor → ~7,000 peak writes/s · ~70,000 peak reads/s

Peak QPS — not average — is what your architecture must survive. Traffic is never uniform across 24 hours.

Deep Dive — The Ratio Trap

Read:Write ratio changes your whole architecture

Social Feed — 100:1 to 1000:1

  • Optimize for READ path
  • Cache + CDN
  • Read replicas
  • Precomputed feeds

Payment Ledger — ~1:1 or write-heavy

  • Optimize for WRITE correctness
  • Write-ahead log
  • Idempotency keys
  • Strong consistency

Common trap: proposing heavy caching for a write-dominated system, or complex write-sharding for a read-dominated one that just needed replicas + cache.
