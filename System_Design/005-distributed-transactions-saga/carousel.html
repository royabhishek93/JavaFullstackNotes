Lost Update & Unrecoverable Compensation

Two Traps

Lost update: Saga A reserves 5 iPhones, Saga B reserves the remaining 5. Saga A's payment fails, "releases" 5 back — but Saga B already shipped its 5. Available=5, physical stock=0. Fix: semantic locking (flag "processing").

Unrecoverable compensation: can't un-ship a package already in transit. Fix: order irreversible steps (shipment, email) LAST.

Decision Tree & Interview Answer

Quick Reference

Simple 2-3 step flow? → Choreography. Complex, needs visibility? → Orchestration. Publishing events from a DB write? → Always use an outbox.

"I never use 2PC for microservices — a coordinator crash leaves all participants holding locks forever. For distributed flows I use Saga: choreography for simple flows, orchestration for complex ones. The transactional outbox is critical — a DB write and a Kafka publish are never atomic on their own. Saga isolation is the hardest trap — fix with semantic locking or by ordering irreversible actions last."
