Cross-Links Kept Out Of The Main ASCII Body To Avoid Duplicate Tables
- Address 1..N -> Orders
- Customer 1..N -> Review
- Restaurant 1..N -> Review
- Orders 0..1 -> Review

Relationship Summary
- Customer 1..N Address
- Customer 1..N Orders
- Address 1..N Orders
- Restaurant 1..N MenuItem
- Restaurant 1..N Orders
- Orders 1..N OrderItem
- MenuItem 1..N OrderItem
- Orders 1..N PaymentAttempt
- DeliveryAgent 1..N DeliveryAssignment
- Orders 0..1 DeliveryAssignment
- Customer 1..N Review
- Restaurant 1..N Review
- Orders 0..1 Review

DB Type Mapping
- PostgreSQL: Customer, Address, Orders, OrderItem, PaymentAttempt, Review, Restaurant, MenuItem, DeliveryAssignment, DeliveryAgent profile
- Redis: DeliveryAgent live location / availability cache

Architect Notes
- PaymentAttempt is modeled as 1..N from Orders to support retries, failures, and gateway callbacks cleanly.
- Review should enforce one review per completed order per customer via UNIQUE(order_id, customer_id).
- PostgreSQL remains the source of truth; Redis is only for fast-changing agent location state.
