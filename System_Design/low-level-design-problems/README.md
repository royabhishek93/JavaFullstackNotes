## Interview Preparation Tips

### Priority Order (Study This Way)
1. **Week 1**: Master Top 5 (Parking Lot, LRU Cache, Elevator, Movie Booking, Splitwise)
2. **Week 2**: Cover Next 5 (Ride Sharing, Hotel Management, Library, Snake & Ladder, ATM)
3. **Week 3**: Domain-specific based on target company (E-commerce, Social, Streaming, etc.)
4. **Day Before**: Review `INTERVIEW_CHEATSHEET.md` thoroughly

### What Interviewers Look For
- ✅ Clear communication of design decisions
- ✅ Understanding of design patterns (Singleton, Factory, Strategy, Observer)
- ✅ Thread safety and concurrency handling
- ✅ Trade-off discussions (time vs space, consistency vs availability)
- ✅ Scalability considerations
- ✅ SOLID principles application
