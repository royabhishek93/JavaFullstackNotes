Two-Level Ranking

Slide 1 / 9 — The Hook

33 min → 150ms

Your best ranking model scores 1ms/item. Run it over 2M candidates and a feed takes 33 minutes to load.

The Hiring Analogy

The Problem

You don't do a 3-hour interview with all 5,000 applicants. A cheap resume screen narrows it to 50 first — THEN the expensive interview happens.

The Funnel

The Solution

2,000,000 candidates
↓ Stage 1: cheap, high-recall
300-500 candidates
↓ Stage 2: expensive, high-precision
Top 20

Stage 1: ANN Retrieval

Optimizes for Recall

Recency filter + ANN embedding search (HNSW). Finds nearest neighbors in latent space WITHOUT scanning all 2M posts — 2-10ms even over 10M vectors.

Stage 2: Rich Re-Ranking

Optimizes for Precision

Feature vector per candidate: recency, predicted engagement, precomputed author-affinity, diversity signals. Only scores the shortlist — 50-100ms.

Decision Tree & Interview Answer

Quick Reference

Candidate pool in the hundreds already? → Just score directly. Millions of candidates? → Two-level funnel is the only way the precise model runs at all.

"You can't run the expensive model over the full candidate pool — the arithmetic doesn't work. I'd split ranking into two stages: cheap high-recall retrieval, then an expensive high-precision model on just the shortlist."
