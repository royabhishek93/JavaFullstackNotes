8-bit logarithmic counter (0–255). Lowest counter evicted first. Counter decays — halved every lfu-decay-time minutes, so old hotness cools off.

Decision Tree & Interview Answer

Quick Reference

Uniform traffic? → allkeys-lru. 1-10% of keys get >80% of traffic? → allkeys-lfu. Short-lived counters? → volatile-ttl. Must never lose data? → volatile-lru + no TTL, or noeviction.

"Use allkeys-lfu when 1% of keys get 99% of traffic — LFU keeps hot keys warm and evicts cold ones; use allkeys-lru for uniform access patterns where recency predicts future use."
