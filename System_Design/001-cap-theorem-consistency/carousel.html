"I'll Use Strong Consistency Everywhere"

The #1 Interview Trap

✕ Performance: 5 replicas to confirm = 5× write latency at 100,000 writes/sec

✕ Availability: 1 replica down = writes blocked entirely (CP behavior)

✕ Unnecessary: follower count off by 1 for 2s? Fine. Bank balance off for 2s? Not fine.

Design consistency level PER FEATURE — not one setting for the whole system.

Decision Tree & Interview Answer

Quick Reference

Is staleness dangerous (money, inventory, auth)? → CP

Is being down worse than being stale (cart, feed, counters)? → AP

"CAP theorem says you can't have Consistency, Availability, and Partition tolerance simultaneously — and since partitions are inevitable, the real choice is CP vs AP. For financial data: CP. For user-experience features: AP. Tunable databases like Cassandra and DynamoDB let you choose per operation. The most common mistake is defaulting to strong consistency everywhere — design consistency per feature instead."
