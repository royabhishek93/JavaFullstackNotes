Decision Tree & Interview Answer

Quick Reference

Scheduled predictable burst (ticket drop, flash sale)? → Waiting room. Organic steady traffic? → Simple rate limiter + autoscaling.

"Rate limiting and a waiting room solve different problems. What I want is admission control with fairness: everyone gets in eventually, in the order they arrived, but the number simultaneously inside the expensive part of the system is capped."
