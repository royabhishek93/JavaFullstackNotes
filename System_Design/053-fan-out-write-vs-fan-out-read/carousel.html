Fan-Out on Write vs Fan-Out on Read

Slide 1 / 10 — The Hook

50,000,000

Cristiano Ronaldo posts a photo. Push it to every follower's feed right now, and that single post queues 50 million Redis writes that take 10–30 minutes to drain. Do it wrong and 50 celebrities posting together means 2.5 billion writes fighting the same queue.

Fan-Out on Write (Push)

Scenario 1 — On Post

50M Redis ZADDs

Post Service inserts into Posts DB + publishes to Kafka → 200 OK instantly. A fan-out worker then pushes post_id into every follower's Redis ZSET, one ZADD feed:{follower_id} at a time.

Fan-Out on Read — The Trap

Scenario 4 — Where It Breaks

500M

queries/sec at just 1M users opening feeds simultaneously (1M × 500 queries each) — ✕ impossible for any database to sustain.

A user following 1,000 accounts triggers 1,000 DB queries per single feed refresh. Without partitioned indexes on (user_id, created_at DESC), even the celebrity-pull path degrades into a full table scan.

Merge Redis ZSET (regular accounts, fanned out on write) with 3–10 direct celebrity queries → sort by timestamp → cache merged result 60s.

Data model: Redis ZSET feed:{user_id} scored by timestamp; Posts partitioned by (user_id, created_at DESC) in Cassandra; separate Following/Followers tables for both directions.

Real World

Where This Pattern Shows Up

Swiggy / Zomato: a restaurant/influencer account with lakhs of followers posting a menu update creates the same write-storm CR7 creates — needs the small-vs-large account split.

Flipkart: a seller account with millions of followers broadcasting a mega-sale update needs fan-out-on-read, while small sellers stay on fan-out-on-write.

PhonePe / Paytm: merchant offer feeds — a small kirana store (300 followers) pushes on write; a national brand (crores of subscribers) must be pulled on read.

Decision Tree & Interview Answer

Slide 10 / 10 — Quick Reference

Poster has <10K followers? → Fan-out on write. ≥10K followers (celebrity)? → Fan-out on read. On every read → merge pre-built Redis feed with live celebrity pulls, cache 60s.

"Fan-out on write pre-builds feeds — great for reads but write-amplification kills you for celebrities. Fan-out on read is write-cheap but read-expensive. The hybrid: fan out small accounts on write, pull celebrity posts on read. Most production social networks do exactly this."
