## 🎯 SECTION 7: DETAILED ANALYSIS

### Why RENT Wins Financially?

1. **Higher Investment Returns**
   - SIP returns: 12% per year
   - Property appreciation: 4% per year
   - Difference: 8% per year compounds over time

2. **Lower Initial Lock-in**
   - Buy: ₹40L locked in property (illiquid)
   - Rent: ₹40L invested from Day 1 (growing at 12%)

3. **Compounding Effect**
   - SIPs grow at 12% on accumulated portfolio
   - Property appreciates only on base value
   - ₹86k monthly SIPs compound over 10 years = ₹2.19 Cr

4. **Opportunity Cost**
   - Money locked in property can't be used elsewhere
   - SIP portfolio remains liquid and accessible
